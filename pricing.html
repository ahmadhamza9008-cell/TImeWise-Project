Questions, answered

What happens if I go over a Free limit?

We will not delete anything. New tasks and steps pause until you remove a few or upgrade to Pro.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. Manage billing opens the Stripe customer portal, where you can cancel or update your card. Pro stays active until the end of the paid period.

Do you store my card details?

No. Stripe handles all payment details. TimeWise only stores your plan status and the Stripe customer identifier.

What if I am just trying TimeWise out?

Local-demo mode unlocks every feature, so you can explore the whole app before connecting an account or a card.
