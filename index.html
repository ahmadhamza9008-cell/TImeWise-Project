Questions

Good to know.

A few things people ask before they start.

Is TimeWise free to use?

Version 1.0 is free while we are in early access. If that changes, existing users will be told well in advance.

Do I need to install anything?

No. TimeWise runs in your browser. A mobile app is planned for later, using the same account and data.

Can I use it for team projects?

The first release focuses on personal planning and focus. Team features are on the roadmap.

What happens to my data?

Your tasks and settings stay tied to your account. You can export or delete them whenever you like.
